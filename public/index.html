About Our School

A School Built on Hard Work, Faith & Family Care

Located in Awaimunga Village, Ocini Parish, Kamdini Sub-county, Oyam District, Olive Nursery and Primary School was founded on the conviction that every child deserves rigorous academic grounding combined with compassionate pastoral care.

🎯 Our Vision

To nurture children who are hardworking, God-fearing, morally upright and resourceful to society.

🚀 Our Mission

To guide children to work very hard and acquire the right knowledge, skills, values and attitude for academic excellence.

Under the leadership of School Director Mr. Bennard Atoo, Managing Director Madam Eveline, and Head Teacher Mr. Denish Odyek, our school keeps small class sizes so every child gets personal care, regular tests to track progress, good warm food, and a clean, safe learning compound.

Character & Conduct

Our Seven Core Values

Seven foundational principles that inspire how pupils, educators, and parents interact, study, and grow together.

🤝

1. Teamwork

Working harmoniously in study groups, co-curriculars, and communal service to achieve collective greatness.

🧗

2. Perseverance

Encouraging tenacity, resilience in challenging topics, and striving continuously until mastery is achieved.

🛡️

3. Trust

Building reliable bonds between educators, children, and families through transparency and accountability.

🙏

4. Respect

Honoring teachers, parents, elders, fellow learners, school regulations, and the natural environment.

❤️

5. Empathy

Understanding and caring for fellow pupils, celebrating triumphs, and supporting those who need encouragement.

🌿

6. Care

Providing safe boarding rooms, clean drinking water, hot nutritious meals, and loving care for every boy and girl.

⚖️

7. Integrity

Practicing honesty in examinations, truthfulness in conduct, and upright moral character at all times.

What Children Learn

From Baby Class to Primary Seven (P.7)

We follow the Uganda Ministry of Education standards with extra care in reading, practical science, sports, debate, and Christian values so every learner succeeds.

Early Childhood Development

Nursery Section (K-1 to K-3)

Under the guidance of Nursery Headmistress Madam Sarah, early learners experience gentle, play-based education.

  • Baby Class (K-1): Phonics, motor skills, sensory play, social habits
  • Middle Class (K-2): Number work, reading readiness, drawing, singing
  • Top Class (K-3): Early reading fluency, writing, English conversation
Foundational Learning

Lower Primary (P.1 – P.3)

Consolidating essential literacy, numeracy, and environmental awareness through interactive teaching methods.

  • Literacy I & II (Reading, creative writing, phonics)
  • Mathematics & Numerical Problem Solving
  • English Language Mastery & Storytelling
  • Local Language & Environmental Social Studies
National Exam Preparation

Upper Primary (P.4 – P.7)

Rigorous subject-specialist preparation overseen by Directors of Studies Mr. Daniel Okullu and Mr. Raimond Onanga.

  • Mathematics & Advanced Problem Solving
  • Integrated Science & Hands-on Laboratory Demonstrations
  • English Language Comprehension & Composition
  • Social Studies (SST) & Religious Education (CRE)
  • Extra PLE revision classes, mock exams, and teacher guidance
Sports, Games & Clubs

Sports, Music, Dance & Clubs

Developing physical fitness, intellectual eloquence, and leadership.

  • Debating & Public Speaking Society (Led by Tr. Raymond & Tr. Monica)
  • Inter-House Athletics, Football, and Netball Tournaments
  • Music, Dance and Drama (MDD) Festivals
  • Young Farmers Agriculture & School Gardening
  • Scripture Union & Moral Guidance Assemblies

Joining Olive School

Admissions for First Term 2027

📅
Official Application Window: Applications for first term 2027 begin on 4th January 2027 and end on 14th February 2027.

Simple 4 Steps to Join

  1. Get Admission Form: Download the printable form (PDF) or apply online between 4th Jan & 14th Feb 2027.
  2. Bring Simple Documents: Child’s birth certificate or baptism card, previous report card (P.1-P.7), and 2 passport photos.
  3. Friendly Meeting: A warm discussion with the Head Teacher to understand your child and recommend the best class.
  4. Report to School: Pay school fees in installments or full, collect uniforms and books, and start learning!

📑 Official Entry Checklist

  • Duly completed and signed Admission Form
  • Photocopy of Child's Birth Certificate or Baptismal Card
  • Two (2) recent passport-sized color photos of the child
  • Previous school academic report cards (for P.1 to P.7)
  • Immunization / health record card photocopy
